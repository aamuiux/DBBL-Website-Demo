## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS, with Framer Motion for component motion, GSAP + ScrollTrigger for scroll storytelling, and Three.js / React Three Fiber only for one or two high-impact moments. Pinned by the user's brief. Runs locally; no deploy target.

## Users

Primary: Gen Z and younger millennials in Bangladesh who live on their phones and judge a bank by its app, its cards and how fast they can move money. Secondary, never alienated: DBBL's existing customers of every age (64M+ per DBBL's homepage), SME owners, corporate treasurers and Bangladeshis abroad sending money home. A visitor usually arrives to do one thing: log in, find an ATM/branch, check a product, get the app, or call 16216.

## Product Purpose

A homepage redesign concept for Dutch-Bangla Bank PLC (DBBL), www.dutchbanglabank.com. It is an unsolicited portfolio / pitch piece, not commissioned work: it uses DBBL's real name, products and verified facts, is clearly labelled as a concept, and runs locally. Success: a first-time visitor understands within seconds that DBBL is the bank that built Bangladesh's digital banking rails, and can reach the right product, the app, or support in one or two moves.

## Positioning

DBBL's truth a neighbour cannot copy: it was the first fully automated bank in Bangladesh (automation completed 2003), launched the country's first mobile financial service (Rocket), the first app-based digital banking solution (NexusPay), runs the largest ATM network in the country (over 4,100 ATMs, per DBBL), and most local banks ride on its shared ATM/card infrastructure. It was Bangladesh's first joint venture bank (with the Dutch development bank FMO) and is the largest bank donor to social causes. Tagline: "Your Trusted Partner". Its story is infrastructure for everyone, not premium exclusivity.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Dutch-Bangla Bank" / DBBL, the existing logo, the line "Your Trusted Partner", and the recognisable DBBL palette (deep blue rgb 44,49,141 used on the current site, alongside the green and red of the logo). Brand recognition must survive the redesign. City Bank (citybankplc.com) is the interaction and craft-level benchmark only, not a look to copy.

Standing preference (chosen by the user over rolled alternatives, 2026-11-21): the homepage plays the category standard straight, a modern consumer-bank homepage, executed at the craft level of City Bank, Revolut and Monzo. No folk, retro or novelty world; distinction comes from DBBL's own truth, palette and execution quality.

Client feedback (2026-11-21): prioritise campaigns, loan products and customer offers; add a dedicated student-account section; highlight Rocket and its features; showcase Fast Track and the ATM network; include Media and CSR sections; lean into Gen Z with modern visuals, motion and prominent colour (full-colour section fields in lime, magenta, green, red and sun yellow on top of the indigo base).

## Product Principles

1. Trust first, Gen Z second: the energy serves clarity and confidence, never competes with them.
2. Show the rails: DBBL's story is the infrastructure it built for everyone; demonstrate it, don't just claim it.
3. One move to the job: login, ATM finder, app download, and 16216 are always within reach.
4. Every number is sourced from DBBL; every invented demo is labelled.
5. Designed for the phone in a hand in Dhaka, then scaled up.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast, keyboard-complete navigation and mega menu, visible focus, reduced-motion support that removes scroll-jacking and parallax, 44px touch targets, semantic landmarks, and readable Bangla typography (a proper Bengali face, not fallback glyphs). Older customers must be as comfortable as younger ones.
