# Surface: DBBL homepage (web/src/app/page.tsx)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Gen Z and young millennials first, every existing DBBL customer never lost. Job: see what DBBL is running right now (campaigns, offers, loans), understand what it offers, reach login / ATM finder / app / 16216 in one move, pick a product by need. Proof: DBBL's own published facts only (see PRODUCT.md Evidence on Hand). Constraints: concept piece, labelled; EN/BN toggle; WCAG 2.2 AA; reduced motion. Revised 2026-11-21 after client feedback (PRODUCT.md): campaigns, loans and offers first; students; Rocket; Fast Track and ATMs; Media; CSR; Gen Z energy with prominent colour.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard played straight, at Revolut/Monzo craft, turned up for Gen Z: a confident consumer bank homepage where DBBL's live campaigns and real firsts do the persuading, in full-colour fields. Refuses the cluttered link-farm portal and the novelty-world gimmick alike.

OWN-WORLD: DBBL indigo (#252A86 / #090B3A) is the base: hero, dark bands, Loans, Media. Full-colour section fields carry the Gen Z energy, one per section, each with a job: lime (#C8F169) = students and the offers ticker, and still the primary action on dark grounds; Rocket magenta (#8B2F8F) = Rocket only; DBBL green (#00904A) = Fast Track, ATMs, CSR, growth; DBBL red (#E31B23) = live campaigns (UDYOG) and alerts; sun (#FFCF3D) = stickers and accents only, never a section ground. Off-white paper (#F5F6FA) and white for calm sections between fields. Bricolage Grotesque display set tight and big, Hanken Grotesk text, Anek Bangla for Bangla. 16-32px radii, pill buttons with arrow chips, sticker badges, ticket-shaped offer cards, real DBBL assets (Fast Track photo, TV-ad posters, billboards) plus labelled stand-in photography, authored phone UI and dial pad.

STORY: The hero's lead slide is the live UDYOG campaign ("Got an idea? Get it funded."), then NexusPay, then the ATM network. Order below the fold: quick actions → offers ticker → Offers & campaigns (UDYOG feature + offer tickets) → Loans → Students (School Savers) → NexusPay pinned story → Rocket → Fast Track & ATM network → product explorer → Cards → video → Media → Segments → Proof → CSR → Help → Footer. The visitor sees what's on now, finds a loan or a first account, sees the apps working, believes the network and the numbers, then logs in, applies, downloads, finds an ATM or Fast Track, or calls 16216.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed photographic hero slider (3 slides: UDYOG campaign, NexusPay, ATM network; crossfade + slow zoom) under a sticky transparent header with mega menu, an "Offers" link with a live dot, and a lime "Internet Banking" pill; headline at 72-96px left-aligned bottom-third, one line of support, primary (lime: "Get the form") + secondary CTA, slide progress rail with pause; a floating quick-action dock (ATM/Branch, Open account, NexusPay, Rocket, 16216) overlaps the hero's bottom edge; the lime offers ticker sits just below it.

FORM: category standard (canon), chosen by the user over the rolled hand; seed:f0aed69d0901b107. Signature interactions: the NexusPay indigo circle spreading from the phone, then four steps on the phone; the video card frame opening to full screen between the split headline. Motion grammar: Lenis smooth scroll, ease-out reveals rising 24px, springy sticker pop-ins and tilts, a draggable offer-ticket rail, the offers marquee, the *322# dial pad typing itself, sticky-header hide-on-down/show-on-up, one Three.js moment (a draggable Nexus card). All of it off under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
